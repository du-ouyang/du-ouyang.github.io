I am **Du Ouyang**, a Ph.D. graduate in Mathematics with Distinction from the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Xiaoqun Wang. I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working with Prof. Yanwei Jia on continuous-time reinforcement learning.

My research focuses on numerical analysis, stochastic control, reinforcement learning, and machine learning. I am especially interested in quasi-Monte Carlo methods, importance sampling, uncertainty quantification, stochastic differential equation simulation, and deep learning methods for high-dimensional partial differential equations.

## Research Interests

- **Numerical analysis:** quasi-Monte Carlo methods, high-dimensional integration, error analysis, and dimensionality reduction.
- **Stochastic control and reinforcement learning:** continuous-time reinforcement learning and stochastic policy approximation.
- **Machine learning for scientific computing:** deep learning methods for nonlinear and fully nonlinear parabolic PDEs.

## Selected Work

- Quasi-Monte Carlo and importance sampling for unbounded integrands, with convergence theory and applications to high-dimensional integration.
- Generalization error analysis for deep backward dynamic programming methods for nonlinear PDEs.
- Accuracy analysis of discretely sampled stochastic policies in continuous-time reinforcement learning.

## Teaching and Service

I served as a teaching assistant for Calculus, Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics, and Probability Theory at Tsinghua University. I also served as captain of the Football Team of the Department of Mathematical Sciences.
